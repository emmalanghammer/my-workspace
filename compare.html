My Workspace

The Express welcome page. Two screens: the original, rebuilt from the production Figma frame, and the proposal, rebuilt on the RMX foundation from the Claude Design “Workspace” canvas. Both ask what the first screen after sign-in should give a property manager — today it is four reference tiles; the proposal keeps Favorites and Reports and adds the work: what is waiting, what is late, and who needs an answer.

    The page as it stands today, rebuilt from the production frame in RMX Pages: My Favorites, My Reports, Announcements and My Training, four Workspace Tiles with coloured overlines. The comparison the proposal below is measured against.

  • My Workspace — proposed

    The whole page, full-bleed and fluid. Live: the announcement carousel, the All / Overdue Toggle Switch, and ticking a task off — the open and overdue counts follow. Everything that goes nowhere says so.
